Privacy Policy

PurrFeed · last updated 8 October 2026

Support Privacy

PurrFeed collects nothing. There is no PurrFeed account, no PurrFeed server, and no analytics. I cannot see your cats, your feeding log, or anything else you put in the app.

That is not a promise about intentions — it is a description of how the app is built. PurrFeed contains no third-party code, makes no network requests of its own, and has nowhere to send anything.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — your cats, their photos, every meal, who logged it, your reminders and your household — is stored on your device and in your own private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. It syncs between your own devices because they share your Apple Account.

I have no access to that database. It belongs to your Apple Account, not to me. Apple's handling of it is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Sharing a household

If you invite someone to your household, Apple's CloudKit sharing puts the shared cats and their feeding log in a shared area that you and the people you invited can read and write. You choose who is invited and you can remove them at any time, which revokes their access. Participants are identified by their Apple Account. Again, this happens inside Apple's infrastructure; it does not pass through any system of mine.

Photos

If you add a photo of a cat, PurrFeed shrinks it and stores it with that cat's record — on your device and in your iCloud, the same as everything else. The app only reads the single image you pick. It does not read, scan or index your photo library.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent from a server, and no push tokens are collected.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or tracking of any kind. PurrFeed does not track you across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app uses only Apple's own frameworks.
  • No contacts, location, microphone, camera or health data.
  • No email address, name or account — there is nothing to sign up for.

Purchases

PurrFeed is unlocked with a single one-time purchase. That transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details, and Apple tells me only anonymous, aggregated sales figures — never who bought it.

Support email

If you write to me for help, I will have whatever you choose to put in that email, including your email address, for as long as it takes to answer you. I do not add it to any list and there is no list to add it to.

Your control

Delete everything
Settings → Delete everything, including iCloud removes your data from the device and from your iCloud, for every device on your Apple Account.
Start over on one device
Settings → Start over on this device clears this device only and leaves iCloud alone.
Take your data with you
Settings → Export feeding log gives you the whole history as a CSV file.
Delete the app
Removing PurrFeed removes the local copy. The iCloud copy is removed from your Apple Account through iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Because I hold none of your data, requests under the GDPR or CCPA to access, correct, export or erase it are things you carry out yourself with the controls above — there is nothing for me to retrieve or delete on your behalf.

Children

PurrFeed is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date above. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new practice.
