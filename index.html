Common questions

My partner can't see the cats

Sharing is iCloud-to-iCloud. Everyone who feeds the cat needs their own Apple device signed into iCloud, and they have to open the invitation you sent from Household → Invite. There is no PurrFeed account and no link a non-Apple user can open, because there is no PurrFeed server — your data lives in your own iCloud.

Nothing is syncing between my iPhone and iPad

Both devices need to be signed into the same Apple Account, with iCloud Drive turned on. Check Settings → Sync details in the app: it says plainly whether iCloud is attached and when it last synced. If it says sync is off, the usual cause is being signed out of iCloud or having iCloud Drive disabled in the system Settings app.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging a meal always succeeds immediately. It syncs on its own once you are back on a network, and conflicts resolve quietly.

How many cats can I add?

As many as live with you. There is no limit and no per-cat charge.

Is the history limited?

No. Every meal is kept for as long as you keep the app, and you can export the lot as a CSV from Settings → Export feeding log.

I paid on one device — do I pay again on another?

No. It is one purchase for your Apple Account, and it is enabled for Family Sharing. If it does not appear on a second device, tap Restore purchase on the paywall.

Do I have to use grams?

Only if you want to. Each cat is set separately — pouches and cups by default, grams if that cat is on a weighed or prescription diet. Treats are always a count.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete everything, including iCloud removes the data from this device and from your iCloud, for every device on your account. It cannot be undone.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 18 or later, signed into iCloud. The Watch app needs a paired Apple Watch. PurrFeed is for Apple devices only — there is no Android or web version, and there is no plan for one, because the app has no server.
